Label-over-value block on the sunken surface. Two uses: the 2-4 column metric grid inside entity cards, and the big count tiles at the top of Summary.

```jsx
<MetricTile label="Last touch" value="18:10:40" mono />
<MetricTile label="Processes" value="200" hint="10 active in current page" icon={<Icon name="layers-3" size={20} />} />
```

Keep labels to two words. Never put a button inside a tile.
